Kevin Song

AI Scientist at AWS

I work on machine learning systems at AWS, where I've led sports analytics initiatives with the NFL and NBA and delivered $20M+ enterprise ML projects. Before AWS, I was a computer vision engineer for the Atlanta Braves and hold a PhD in biophysics from the University of Chicago.

Projects

  • NFL Helmet Impact Detection Computer Vision

    Computer vision pipeline that detects helmet collisions in video and assigns each impact to individual players — producing the first comprehensive dataset of NFL helmet impacts.

  • NFL Coverage Assignment Models Sports ML

    Three factorized attention-based transformer models classifying individual coverage assignments, defender-receiver matchups, and target defenders from multi-agent trajectory data. Used in NFL broadcast.

  • NBA Play Segmentation Sports ML

    Temporal DETR-based transformer model for detecting play boundaries from player tracking and game event data.

  • NBA Defensive Pressure & Gravity Sports ML

    Factorized attention-based transformer models computing expected and actual defensive pressure scores, used to quantify player gravity in NBA broadcasts.

  • ADP Assist Intent Orchestration Generative AI

    GenAI-based synthetic utterance generation pipeline with embedding semantic similarity retrieval, achieving 95%+ routing accuracy for multi-agent orchestration.
